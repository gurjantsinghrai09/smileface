# ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Not submitting to Google Search Console**
   - Without this, Google doesn't know about your site
   - It takes longer to get indexed
   
2. **Ignoring analytics**
   - You can't improve what you don't measure
   - Analytics shows what's working

3. **Not updating content**
   - Old, outdated content ranks worse
   - Monthly updates signal freshness to Google

4. **Keyword stuffing**
   - Write naturally for humans first
   - Google penalizes keyword stuffing

5. **Ignoring mobile users**
   - 60%+ of traffic is mobile
   - Mobile experience is critical

6. **Poor page speed**
   - Page speed affects both UX and rankings
   - Compress images and enable caching

7. **No internal linking**
   - Link to other relevant pages
   - Helps with crawling and user navigation

8. **Thin content**
   - 300 words is bare minimum
   - 1000+ words ranks better
   - Quality > Quantity
